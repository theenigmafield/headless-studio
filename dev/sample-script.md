# Sample reel: reading script

> Read straight through in one take. Directions are not read aloud.

> Warm and close, like you are talking to **one** person.

starts the same way.

and the day is ==already== asking.
(breathe)

## Line 3

(breathe)

Just breathe,
and *begin*.
